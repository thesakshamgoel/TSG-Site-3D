2026-11-07 (scroll bounce)
- Fix: on touch screens the page no longer nudges a scroll that comes to rest
  inside Featured; that nudge pulled the page back toward the previous film
  and read as a bounce (measured: 14 backwards frames in 14 swipes before,
  0 after). Touch scrolling is fully native; the film follows where you stop.
- Tried overscroll-behavior: none and overflow-anchor: none; the first blocked
  touch scrolling at the end of Featured in testing, so both were removed.
- Removed the unused useSmoothScroll hook.

2026-11-07 (mobile scroll correction)
- Touch scrolling is native again. Driving it from the page (the previous
  change) made it slower and laggier on a real phone. Lenis now smooths only
  the mouse wheel.
- Grade shader renders at 1x on phones (was 1.25x): fewer pixels per frame.

2026-11-07 (mobile pass)
- Credentials on phones and tablets: a swipeable row, one card at a time with
  the next peeking, a 01 / 05 counter and tap targets for each card. Desktop
  keeps the grid.
- Mobile lag: the Grade section decoded twelve large frames, drew them into
  atlases and uploaded them in one burst, freezing the page (1.2 s in a 4x
  CPU-throttled test). It now loads one tile every ~90 ms, off the main thread,
  with smaller tiles and a lower render scale on phones (worst frame now
  ~0.2 s in the same test). The intro loader uses 8 film frames instead of 14
  on phones.
- Mobile scroll speed: touch scrolling is now driven by the page (finger 0.9x,
  capped inertia) instead of the phone's native fling, so a flick no longer
  tears through several sections.
- Reel heading fits the screen ("projects. One eye." was clipped).
- Cookie card on phones is one short line of text with the two answers side by
  side underneath.
